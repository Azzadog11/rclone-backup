Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

  • This app is used only by its owner, to back up their own files to their own Google Drive.
  • It uses the drive.file permission: it can read and write only the Google Drive files it created itself. It cannot see any other files.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. Google and anyone else see only encrypted data.
  • No personal data is collected, shared, sold or sent anywhere other than the owner's Google Drive.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
